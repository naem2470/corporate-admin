# Org Chart

Canonical roster of roles. Update this file whenever a role changes hands or
a new department is added.

| Role | Filled By | Reports To | Department Folder |
|------|-----------|------------|--------------------|
| Founder | Founder | N/A | `../departments/finance/` (and all departments) |

## Adding a Role

1. Add a row above.
2. If the role needs its own department workflow, copy
   `../departments/_template/` to `../departments/[new-department]/` and
   fill it in.

## Role Skill & Integration Assignments

Tier 3 of both the skills and integrations three-tier systems. A
role-scoped skill or integration still lives in the company or department
folder that owns it; this table just says which seat may use it, by name,
not by path. For integrations, this is the access-policy layer standing
in for the per-directory config Claude Code does not support: which seat
may touch the bank account is a real question even though nothing here
can enforce it technically.
